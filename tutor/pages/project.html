Polyvault: a 3D asset store

A small but complete online store, written in Python with FastAPI. Browse 3D models, animations and worlds, add them to a cart, check out (pretend payment) and download what you bought from your profile.

  • Inside: web routes, page templates, a SQLite database, login and a shopping cart.
  • You'll rebuild: the login logic first, then the cart.
  • Demo login: learner / carve123
